# Weekly Summary November 21 to 27

Scope: MCSD-TP, P2GD-type near-region repairs, returned-step diagnostics and
their width256 LLM transfer. This is a rolling seven-day summary, not a list
of every unrelated server job. Historical completed 1B/3B benchmarks are
references, not new results from this week.

## Main Conclusion

**A numerical-cluster P2GD-type direction now has promising cumulative
evidence, but not a demonstrated production advantage over MuonSphere/SSO.**
Single-step replays did not isolate a multidirectional benefit. Two subsequent
256-update panels favored cluster repair after BF16 returned-step calibration.
They use different starting points of the same seed, not two independent seeds.
The new cheap 3B transfer is running and is too early to rank.

## Evidence Chain

| Study | What it tested | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Local small spectral spheres | Six directions, full return, matched spectral/output movement | Cluster can improve on ambient/top-1, but not universally on half-D0; numerical clusters can stagnate in constructed examples |
| Early six-control LLM replay | 24 informative same-state updates | Cluster did not beat ambient/top-1; output attenuation reproduced 98.6% of its mean gain over half-D0 |
| Later BF16-matched replay | 14 informative states, all top2 | Tiny advantage over ambient, but not top-1 or output attenuation |
| Five independent paths, start512 | 256 committed updates per policy | Cluster best endpoint and last-four mean |
| Four independent paths, start768 | 256 committed updates per policy | Cluster again best endpoint and last-four mean |
| Shared-AUS trajectories | Six 64-update Muon-direction/MCSD-TP arms | Muon-direction wins at all three matched angular targets; no P2GD arm |
| Warm K10 3B transfer | Cluster top6 / top1 / ambient | Two running, one queued; initial same-step validation favors cluster |

Same-state replay candidates are restored after evaluation; they are not six
independent training runs. Local records and repeated held-out evaluations
are likewise not independent initialization seeds.

## What Changed Our Interpretation

- **Actual movement matters.** FP32 matching left up to6.89% BF16 budget
  discrepancy in the early cluster replay. Direct BF16 matching reduced the
  maximum below0.5% in both trajectory panels, though stricter internal search
  tolerance failures were retained and reported.
- **Top4 can omit useful directions.** It misses9.54% and4.85% of observed
  cluster calls in the two paths. Top6 covers the later panel, but the earlier
  one contains nine size7 calls. Top6 is a practical test, not guaranteed
  complete geometry.
- **Repeated near calls are not automatically bad.** Cluster had92.4%--93.2%
  consecutive-near fractions while achieving the best loss. Repetition is a
  diagnostic of residence, not an optimization-quality metric by itself.
- **More complexity is not automatically useful.** Gap-preserving finite-step
  repair did not improve the six-way LLM replay; it has not earned a larger
  training sweep this week.
- **Numerical clusters are not exact repeated singular values.** Local
  counterexamples show why treating separated values as ties can stagnate.
  Fixed K10, top6 truncation and approximate radial return do not inherit
  exact projection-P2GD's convergence conclusions.

## ELR and State Feedback

The six-arm early trajectory study matches a common FP32-master **angular
chord**, not BF16 spectral displacement or output ELR. The Muon-direction
reference uses the same exact radial return as MCSD-TP; it is **not native
MuonSphere's PI-based update**.

At angular multiplier1.00, final loss is4.499633 for Muon-direction and
4.502379 for MCSD-TP. At0.95, it is4.476445 and4.511190. Last16 means favor
Muon-direction at all three multipliers. A smaller angular budget helps the
Muon-direction arm here; 0.95 is the lowest tested value, not an optimum.

At multiplier1, sampled MCSD-TP spectral movement is5.78% larger despite
nearly identical mean BF16 angular movement. On each algorithm's own states:

| Sampled median | Muon-direction | MCSD-TP |
|---|---:|---:|
| Relative top-two gap | 0.034707 | 0.004347 |
| Trial spectral perturbation / absolute gap | 0.433 | 4.033 |
| First-order radial-model residual | 1.08% | 8.44% |

This residual is **not SVD error or sphere violation**. In contrast, applying
four directions to identical Muon snapshots gives median full-step residuals
around1.05%--1.21%, which roughly halve when the coefficient halves.
The larger discrepancy develops along MCSD-TP's own states. A direction/state
feedback mechanism is plausible, but neither correlation nor Muon-only
snapshot replay establishes causality.
