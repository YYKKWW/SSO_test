## Consistency and intentional differences

Submission preflight compared the resolved model, training/data/environment,
numerics and budget fields across all ten runs. They match except for method,
geometry and the explicitly listed method-specific momentum below. Actual
submissions must also match this frozen configuration before interpreting losses.

| Methods | Momentum beta | Nesterov |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| MCSD, MCSD-TP, iMuon adaptation | 0.95 | No |
| MuonH, SSO, MuonSphere adaptations | 0.9 | Yes |

MCSD/TP use the common Polar Express LMO (10 steps). Native spectral baseline
kernels retain NS8 and PI10; SSO retains tolerance 2e-4 and at most 20
bisections. These numerical and momentum differences are intentional defaults,
not a claim of identical update rules. The baselines share the new logical
matrix split, initialization, fixed radii/shape scales and auxiliary routing;
they are matched adaptations, not untouched author recipes. In contrast,
MCSD versus MCSD-TP within a geometry matches the non-method settings.

This pilot is an application/stability comparison, not a pure tangent-
projection ablation and not a tuned ranking. Follow-up attribution requires
matched momentum/numerics; final quality comparisons require equal tuning
budgets and paired seeds. A common LR alone does not establish optimal tuning.

## Comparison boundaries and next gate

The old width-256, sequence-4096, head-wise-split 3B runs are a separate
protocol. Their losses must not be merged with this width-384, sequence-2048,
GQA, whole-logical-matrix table. Re-running SSO/MuonSphere here provides the
appropriate within-project reference. Across different geometries the initial
feasible weights/function classes also differ; prioritize within-geometry
comparisons rather than attributing all cross-geometry differences to directions.

After each pilot, check full completion, NaN/skips, constraint and LMO audits,
train/validation curves, peak memory and steady-state time separately from
startup/evaluation. Near repeated spectral values are logged, not rejected.
Only after these checks select equal-budget LR tests and then paired-seed 3B
runs. A loss at 100M does not establish the ranking at 3B.
